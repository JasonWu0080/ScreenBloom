# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/) as its code of conduct. Everyone who participates — in issues, pull requests, discussions or any other project space — is expected to follow it.

In short:

- Be respectful and welcoming. Disagree about ideas, not people.
- No harassment, insults, discriminatory language, sexualized content, or publishing others' private information.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully, and focus on what is best for the project and its users.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behavior to the project maintainers at **1156872601@qq.com**. Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers may remove comments, commits or other contributions that violate this code, and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors, following the enforcement guidelines of the Contributor Covenant.
